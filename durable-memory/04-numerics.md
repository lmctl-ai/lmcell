# Numerical methods and independent oracles

The adapter uses PhysiCell Cell objects, the upstream single-phase live cycle, stochastic Death::check_for_death and apoptosis clearance, and Cell::update_motility_vector. Its own coordinator applies builder-defined multiplicative Hill feedback, creates a colocated daughter on the native cycle flag, and uses Euler positions with reflecting walls. It deliberately omits mechanical interactions and continuous volume growth. Uniform subdivisions give a constant actual timestep duration/ceil(duration/max_dt), avoiding upstream solver caches retaining an earlier dt for a partial final step.

BioFVM's implicit 2D LOD solver advances diffusion/decay after cell secretion/uptake. Recomputing uptake constants prevents stale coefficients after apoptosis. Spherical cell volume divided by voxel volume scales exchange. Secretion saturation is 1. Fixed edge values are imposed at edge voxel centers between sweeps, with a finite-step boundary error. Convergence requires reducing dt and spacing; the 1D profile test uses dt=.01, dx=1, D=1 and decay=.01, t=1000.

For pure birth E[N]=N0 exp(rt) and variance=N0 exp(rt)(exp(rt)-1). For death E[N]=N0 exp(-dt) and variance=N0 p(1-p). Tests start with 10000 independent agents and compare to closed forms with three standard errors plus an explicit small-step allowance. A half-saturated decreasing apoptosis rule halves the rate. On Linux the seeded populations are growth 18225 vs 18221.2, death 5420 vs 5488.12, rules 7402 vs 7408.18.

The 2D persistent walk has D=v^2*tau/2 and continuous MSD=2*v^2*tau*(t-tau*(1-exp(-t/tau))). Native discrete correlation is q^k with q=1-dt/tau, hence MSD=v^2*dt^2*[n+2*sum((n-k)*q^k)]. At v=1,tau=1,t=20,dt=.05, 6000 walkers yield MSD 37.5563, discrete expectation 37.1 and measured standard error .453933. Separate x/y mean checks assert isotropy. For t much greater than tau this approaches 4Dt.

Steady D*c''-lambda*c=0 with equal boundaries is cosh(k*(x-L/2))/cosh(k*L/2), k=sqrt(lambda/D). Far from the opposite boundary it approaches exp(-k*x). The native test checks every profile point and exponential penetration ten voxels from the left boundary. Uniform substrate decay checks exp(-lambda*t). Uptake in one voxel checks exp(-uptake*Vcell/Vvoxel*t). These are independent formulas, not reference values generated by the implementation.


Secretion/uptake follows BioFVM's implicit step rho'=(rho+a*S)/(1+a*(S+U)), a=dt*Vcell/Vvoxel, saturation 1; the native secretion scenario matches that recurrence to 1e-12 and the continuous relaxation .75*(1-exp(-c*(S+U)*t)) to 2e-4. Rule factors are 1+f (increases) and 1-f (decreases) of the base rate, f the Hill response; the increase scenario checks growth at 1.5x the cycle rate (24522 vs 24596) and the speed scenario a quartered walk MSD (9.10 vs 9.275, SE .137). A no-flux substrate beside a Dirichlet substrate stays exactly uniform.

Sources: [PhysiCell paper](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pcbi.1005991), [BioFVM paper](https://doi.org/10.1093/bioinformatics/btv730), [pinned source](https://github.com/MathCancer/PhysiCell/tree/dbd3499250141b27600e91e501c54c46f68f2763).

## Fixed edges, substeps and new oracles (2026-10-23)

BioFVM's LOD solver treats an edge voxel as unknown during each 1D sweep and re-pins it afterwards, so the steady profile next to a fixed edge sits low by about 0.05*D*dt/h^2 relative (measured: linear 0..1 profile over 190 um, D=20, h=10, dt=1 gave 0.0619 instead of 0.0526 at the second voxel; at D*dt/h^2 = 0.01 the error is below 2e-3). Uptake-dominated fields have the analogous split error in uptake*dt (spheroid probe: centre 0.033/0.055/0.118 at dt 0.01/0.05/0.2 with real-oxygen rates). `diffusion_dt` fixes both without shrinking the cell step.

New native oracles (tests/scientific.cpp): `edges` 2D Laplace on a 21x21 square, one side 1 and three 0: centre 0.24997 vs 1/4 by superposition, mirror symmetry to 1e-9, quarter points vs the sinh Fourier series within 0.01 (dt=0.025, D=10, h=5). `gradient` linear profile between fixed xmin/xmax within 2e-3, and again with dt=1 plus diffusion_dt=0.05. `chemotaxis`: persistence < dt redraws the heading every step; mean drift speed is v*E[(b+(1-b)cos t)/|b e+(1-b)u|] = v for b=1 (150.0 vs 150) and 2v/pi for b=1/2 (95.54 vs 95.49); no drift across the gradient. `toward`: zero base cycle, saturation 0.3 at half-max grows as exp(0.15 t). `crowding`: one voxel, cycle 0.1*(1-H(N-1)), death 0.02, K=50, n=2: time-mean population 100.996 vs N* = 1 + K*(r/d-1)^(1/n) = 101. `multiexchange`: one cell takes up food (exp(-2c t)) and secretes signal (1-exp(-3c t)) at once.
